We get to know you, discuss your goals, preferences, and ideas. We gather information about your event. UX/UI and Visual Design

We work on the conceptual content of future design solutions, considering user interaction logic. We select colors, font pairs, and create graphic elements to enhance the previously established meanings with relevant visuals. We create adaptive design layouts.

Development

We bring design layouts to life with code, create interactive animations where needed, and adapt the site for different screen types. We make necessary integrations: CRM, chatbot, or anything else you need. We ensure everything works like clockwork: adaptation, loading speed. We connect the site to your domain and publish it.

Guarantee support

After the release, we provide one month of warranty support to ensure everything runs smoothly. We can also make content updates or additional changes on an hourly basis, such as updating event programs, adding new speakers, or other modifications as needed.
